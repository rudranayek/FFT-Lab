FFT Image Lab

Image → 2D Fourier spectrum (color or grayscale), and spectrum → original image. Runs fully in your browser.

1. Image → FFT

Step 1: upload an image. Step 2: pick size, color mode and spectrum type. Step 3: click Compute FFT.

"Magnitude + Phase" gives a PNG with magnitude (left) and phase (right) (colored if you chose RGB, since each color channel gets its own FFT), which is what Section 2 needs. Magnitude-only and phase-only are for viewing and cannot rebuild the image.

2. FFT → Image (inverse FFT)

Step 1: upload a spectrum file. Step 2: pick the spectrum type. Step 3: click Compute inverse FFT.

Spectrum type
Uploaded spectrum
Recovered image

Color is detected automatically (color spectrum gives a color image). Only the first two types rebuild the original image. Magnitude-only and phase-only give a normalized image for experimenting.

Note: the PNG spectrum must not be resized, cropped or compressed (JPEG) before uploading.
